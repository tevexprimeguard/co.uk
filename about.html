OUR STORY

Built on a Single Principle

Founded in 1997, Tevex Prime Guard emerged from a simple belief: the world’s most valuable assets deserve absolute security without compromise. What began as a private vault for family heirlooms has grown into a global leader in high‑security storage, cyber vaults, and secure logistics.

We combine military‑grade physical protection with cutting‑edge digital encryption, serving high‑net‑worth families, Fortune 500 corporations, and government agencies. Discretion is our cornerstone.
